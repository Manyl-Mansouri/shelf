# Third-party artwork

- **Inter** typeface — The Inter Project Authors, SIL Open Font License 1.1 (`Inter-OFL.txt`).
- **PS5 wordmark** (`logo_ps5.png`), from "PlayStation 5 logo and wordmark.svg", Wikimedia Commons. Trademark of Sony Interactive Entertainment; used here only to label the PS5 input on a personal launcher.
- **Nintendo Switch mark** (`logo_switch.png`), from "Nintendo Switch Logo (without text).svg", Wikimedia Commons. Trademark of Nintendo.
- **PlayStation 5 photo** (`hero_ps5.png`), "PlayStation 5 and DualSense with transparent background.png" by Soberian, Wikimedia Commons, CC BY-SA 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). Cropped and resized.
- **Nintendo Switch photo** (`hero_switch.png`), "Nintendo-Switch-wJoyCons-BlRd-Standing-FL.png" by Evan-Amos, Wikimedia Commons, public domain. Cropped and resized.
- Show backgrounds and logos are loaded at runtime from Metahub (via Cinemeta); album covers from Apple's public Marketing Tools charts feed. Nothing from either is bundled.
- **YouTube logo** (`logo_youtube.png`), from "YouTube Logo 2017.svg", Wikimedia Commons, with the wordmark set in white for dark backgrounds. Trademark of Google; used only to label YouTube's Top Shelf.
- Game box art for the PS5 and Switch walls is loaded at runtime from Wikipedia (non-free cover art, fetched like a browser would); nothing is bundled.
